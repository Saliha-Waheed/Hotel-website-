✦ Our Story

Luxury Redefined Since 2012

Founded in 2010, Hotel Lux has been providing exceptional hospitality experiences to travelers from around the world. Our commitment to luxury, comfort, and personalized service has made us a preferred destination for both business and leisure travelers.

With 150 beautifully appointed rooms, world-class dining options, and state-of-the-art facilities, we ensure every stay is memorable.
